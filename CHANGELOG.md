# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-30
- initial release
- keeps your chosen companion pet summoned: re-summons it when you start moving if it isn't already out
- does not re-summon while in combat, dead/ghost, or mounted
- per-character pet selection (saved per character)
- Rematch integration: right-click a pet > "Keep Out Permanently" to choose your pet, with a marker icon on the pet in the list
- without Rematch: Alt+click a pet in the Blizzard Pet Journal, marked with a small overlay icon
- `/mc status` and `/mc clear` slash commands
