# 🐾 Mini-Companion

**Mini-Companion** is a lightweight World of Warcraft addon that keeps your chosen companion pet summoned. Pick a pet once, and the addon makes sure it stays out with you while you adventure.

Inspired by an old WeakAura that re-summoned your pet whenever you started moving — rebuilt as a proper addon with a clean way to choose your pet directly from the Pet Journal.

---

## Features

**🐾 Keep Your Pet Out**
Whenever you start moving and your chosen pet isn't already summoned, Mini-Companion summons it for you — so your favorite companion is always at your side.

**🛡️ Stays Out of the Way**
Never re-summons while you're in combat, dead or a ghost, or mounted. It only acts when it's safe and sensible to do so.

**👤 Per-Character Selection**
Each character remembers its own chosen pet, so your warrior and your mage can each keep their own companion.

**✨ Rematch Integration**
If you use [Rematch](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rematch), right-click any pet and choose **Keep Out Permanently**. The chosen pet is marked with a small icon in the list.

**📜 Works Without Rematch**
No Rematch? Alt+click a pet in the Blizzard Pet Journal to choose it. The selected pet gets a small marker icon, similar to the favorite star.

---

## Usage

- **With Rematch:** right-click a pet → **Keep Out Permanently** (right-click again to clear).
- **Without Rematch:** Alt+click a pet in the Pet Journal.
- `/mc status` — show the currently chosen pet.
- `/mc clear` — clear the chosen pet.
